# 2. BRAND GUIDELINES

## Brand Identity
*   **Mission:** To provide the world with uncompromising privacy without sacrificing the power of modern AI productivity.
*   **Vision:** A future where digital communication is inherently safe, lightning-fast, and intelligently augmented on the user's terms.
*   **Core Values:** Cryptographic security, architectural transparency, seamless performance, and aesthetic minimalism.
*   **Brand Philosophy:** "Silent Security, Intelligent Assistance."

## Brand Personality
The brand must feel **Futuristic, Intelligent, Calm, Secure, Minimal, Trustworthy,** and **Modern**. It operates like a high-end digital concierge inside a secure vault.

## Tone of Voice
*   **Messaging Tone:** Confident, concise, and professional. 
*   **UX Writing Tone:** Instructional and reassuring. **Avoid corporate stiffness and manipulative engagement tactics** (e.g., no "You're missing out!" notifications).
*   **Notification Tone:** Unobtrusive and context-aware. 
*   **AI Assistant Tone:** Helpful, objective, and neutral. It must **strictly avoid creepy, overly familiar, or anthropomorphic AI behavior**. It is a tool, not a friend.
